## Before publishing
1. Confirm that `https://www.kalro-dairy-institute.com/` is the correct live domain and that you control it. The canonical URL and sitemap currently use this domain because it appears in the supplied reference catalogue.
2. Confirm the displayed prices, units, livestock descriptions, stock, and delivery terms with the responsible seller before publishing. The catalogue uses the reference prices; prices and stock can change.
3. Confirm the business is authorized to use KALRO's name, logo, and institutional identity. Do not describe the website as an official KALRO website or imply government endorsement unless this is authorized.
4. Verify the site in Google Search Console, submit `/sitemap.xml`, and inspect the canonical URL.
5. Test the mobile layout, WhatsApp links, order quantity calculator, appointment form, and all product images after uploading to the live host.

## Google Ads launch checklist
- Start with a Search campaign and tightly themed ad groups: dairy cattle, goats and sheep, animal feeds and fodder, poultry chicks, and pasture seeds/grass.
- Use exact and phrase match keywords first. Add negative keywords for unrelated searches (for example: jobs, careers, free, definition, school assignment, and used equipment where irrelevant).
- Send each ad group to the most relevant section of the website rather than using one generic ad for every search.
- Use a location setting that matches actual service and delivery coverage. Do not target all of Kenya unless nationwide enquiries can be served.
- Use call assets and sitelinks only with verified phone numbers and real pages/sections.
- To optimize for leads, install the Google tag and configure Google Ads conversion actions for WhatsApp clicks, telephone clicks, and successful appointment enquiries. No tracking ID has been added because the account's Google tag / conversion ID was not supplied.
- Review search terms, conversion quality, cost per qualified lead, and impression share. Targeting Absolute Top Impression Share can increase cost and does not guarantee the first ad position.
